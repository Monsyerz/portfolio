Architectural Glass Enclosures

Shop drawing work for custom glass enclosures, connecting design intent with hardware, field conditions and installation details.

The problem

Communicate a custom glass enclosure in relation to openings, adjacent finishes and hardware. Plans alone do not show every connection or installation condition.

My role

I prepared plans, elevations and detail drawings, coordinated hardware requirements and incorporated revisions into drawing packages.

Drawing decisions

The selected sheets pair plans with elevations, call out hardware and show connection details separately. This makes the enclosure arrangement and the interfaces at the top, bottom and door edge readable at different scales.

The result

The selected drawing excerpts show enclosure layouts, hardware references and connection details. They document the drafting output; installation completion, client acceptance and measured cost or time savings are not claimed.
