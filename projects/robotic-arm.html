ACADEMIC PROJECT · MECHANICAL DESIGN

Robotic Arm

A conceptual robotic arm designed in Autodesk Inventor, from individual components to an assembly and technical views.

The problem

Represent a mechanical arm concept at both component and assembly level, then communicate a part through 2D technical views.

My role

I modeled individual components in Autodesk Inventor, brought them together in an assembly and prepared 2D views. This was an academic design and documentation project.

Design and documentation decisions

The work separates the component model, overall assembly and orthographic views. The assembly shows the relationship between parts; the component and drawing views expose geometry that is harder to read in the assembled model.

The result

The deliverables shown here are a component model, an assembly view and a technical drawing excerpt. They demonstrate modeling and documentation work; no physical prototype, load capacity or measured performance is claimed.
